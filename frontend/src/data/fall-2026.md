# Fall 2026 Program

**Split:** Upper/Lower A/B/C · 4–5 days/week

**Schedule (4-day):** Mon Lower A · Tue Upper A · Thu Lower B · Fri Upper B

**Schedule (5-day):** Sun Upper C-5 · Mon Lower A · Tue Upper A · Thu Lower B · Fri Upper B

**Core:** 10 min after Lower A and Upper B (see Core section under Notes)

**Intensity:** RIR = reps in reserve. Compounds: RIR 2. Isolations: RIR 1.

**5s PRO** applies to Bench and Squat only. Run 2 cycles of the 3-week wave, then deload.

- Week 1: 65/75/85% × 5/5/5 · Week 2: 70/80/90% × 5/5/5 · Week 3: 75/85/95% × 5/5/5
- All sets are 5 reps — no AMRAP. Stop each set cleanly, do not grind.
- After the top set, move straight into accessories.
- *Optional back-off:* 1 x 8–12 at ~65% of TM after the top set. Week 1 (65/75/85) is a weak hypertrophy stimulus on its own and this closes the gap. **Drop it during a cut** — it adds fatigue to your two heaviest lifts when recovery is limited.

**Progression (all other lifts):** double progression — add reps until the top of the range, then add load and reset to the bottom.

**Deload:** every 6 weeks (after 2 full 5s PRO cycles), or earlier if performance drops 2+ consecutive weeks. Cut sets ~40%, keep loads. Target RIR 4–5 across all exercises — sessions should feel almost too easy. For 5s PRO sets, drop to ~40% and focus on clean, controlled reps.

## Upper A — Strength + Hypertrophy


| Exercise                                       | Sets  | Reps          | Suggested Rest |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----- | ------------- | -------------- |
| Flat Barbell Bench Press                       | 5s PRO | —            | 3–4 min        |
| Weighted Pull-Up                               | 4     | 6–10 (RIR 2)  | 90 sec         |
| Seated Cable Row                               | 3     | 8–12 (RIR 2)  | 90 sec         |
| Seated DB Shoulder Press                       | 3     | 8–12 (RIR 2)  | 90 sec         |
| Cable Lateral Raise (or DB lateral raise)      | 4     | 12–15 (RIR 1) | 60 sec         |
| Incline Cable Fly (or incline pec deck)        | 2     | 10–12 (RIR 1) | 60 sec         |
| EZ Bar Skullcrusher                            | 4     | 8–12 (RIR 1)  | 60 sec         |
| EZ Bar Curl (or DB curl)                       | 4     | 10–12 (RIR 1) | 60 sec         |


- *Superset: EZ Bar Skullcrusher + EZ Bar Curl — do back to back, rest after the curl. Same bar.*
- *EZ Bar Skullcrusher: the bar must travel **past/behind the head**, upper arms angled back ~30° — not vertical. A skullcrusher to the forehead with a vertical upper arm trains the long head in a shortened position and is barely different from a pushdown; the behind-the-head path is the entire point of this exercise. EZ bar, not straight. Control the eccentric. Build up: 3 sets for the first two weeks, then 4. If elbows complain, swap to Overhead Cable Extension — same long-head emphasis, gentler joint.*
- *Weighted Pull-Up: a dip belt solves loading at any gym. If weight is genuinely unavailable, substitute heavy Lat Pulldown 4 x 6–10 rather than dropping to bodyweight — unweighted 6–10 is far from failure if you can do 12+ clean reps, and it duplicates the Upper B pull-up.*
- *Seated Cable Row: use a close or neutral grip, pull to lower abdomen with elbows tucked close to your sides — think "elbow to hip" to shift emphasis toward the lats.*

## Lower B — Hypertrophy


| Exercise                                            | Sets | Reps             | Suggested Rest |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| Pendulum Squat                                      | 3    | 8–12 (RIR 2)     | 2 min          |
| Seated Leg Curl                                     | 3    | 10–12 (RIR 1)    | 90 sec         |
| Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (DB or KB)             | 2    | 8–10/leg (RIR 2) | 90 sec         |
| Leg Extension (or cable leg extension)              | 3    | 10–12 (RIR 1)    | 60 sec         |
| Seated Calf Raise (or seated DB calf raise)         | 3    | 15–20 (RIR 1)    | 60 sec         |


- *Pendulum Squat: face toward the machine for maximum quad emphasis and depth. If unavailable, substitute in this order: (1) Hack Squat facing away, (2) Front Squat, (3) Heel-elevated Goblet Squat. Note: goblet squat has a load ceiling — use it as a last resort only.*
- *Hack Squat: use the **45° angled sled** machine (looks like an inverted leg press — angled rails, back pad, shoulder pads). Avoid the vertical/V-squat version if you have a choice; skip Smith-machine "hack squats" entirely. Setup: feet low on the platform and fairly close together, full depth, lower back flat against the pad, slow eccentric. Same 3 x 8–12 RIR 2 — loads will not transfer numerically from the pendulum.*
- *If leg curl machine is unavailable, substitute with Nordic Curl 2 x 5–8 (RIR 2). Note: Nordics cause significant DOMS early on — start at 3–4 reps, use a 3–5 sec eccentric, and build slowly. Avoid pushing these hard before a long run week.*
- *Single-leg RDL: use a weight that lets you hinge with control and balance. If balance is the limiter, use a rack or wall for light fingertip support.*
- *If leg extension machine is unavailable, add an extra set of Pendulum Squat, Hack Squat, or Leg Press instead.*
- *Superset: Leg Extension + Seated Calf Raise — do back to back, rest after the calf raise.*

## Upper B — Hypertrophy


| Exercise                                      | Sets | Reps          | Suggested Rest |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---- | ------------- | -------------- |
| Incline DB Press                              | 4    | 8–12 (RIR 2)  | 90 sec         |
| Pull-Up                                       | 3    | 8–12 (RIR 2)  | 90 sec         |
| Chest-Supported DB Row (or single-arm DB row) | 3    | 10–12 (RIR 2) | 90 sec         |
| Incline Cable Fly (or incline pec deck)       | 3    | 10–12 (RIR 1) | 60 sec         |
| Cable Face Pull                               | 3    | 12–15 (RIR 1) | 60 sec         |
| Cable Lateral Raise (or DB lateral raise)     | 4    | 12–15 (RIR 1) | 60 sec         |
| Overhead Tricep Extension (DB or cable)       | 2    | 10–12 (RIR 1) | 60 sec         |
| Hammer Curl                                   | 3    | 10–12 (RIR 1) | 60 sec         |


- *Monitor total pressing fatigue across the week — if anterior shoulder discomfort develops, reduce Incline DB Press volume first.*
- *Incline Cable Fly: use the low pulley, cables set at the bottom, pulling upward and inward toward your upper chest. If using a pec deck, set the seat low so arms angle upward.*
- *Fly execution: set the elbow at ~15° of bend and **lock it there** — if the elbow angle changes through the rep you are pressing, not flying. Movement happens at the shoulder only. Hands finish at chin/forehead height, not at the belly. 2–3 second eccentric; the loaded stretch at the bottom is the whole exercise. If the front delt or biceps tendon fails before the pec, it is too heavy. Reassess in 4 weeks — if the pec still is not what gives out, swap to Incline DB Fly or a low-seat pec deck.*
- *If you want to do Dips, slot them after Incline DB Press or swap with Incline Cable Fly.*
- *Superset A: Cable Face Pull + Cable Lateral Raise — do back to back, rest after the lateral raise.*
- *Superset B: Overhead Tricep Extension + Hammer Curl — do back to back, rest after the curl.*

## Cut Block — Nov 2026 or post-holidays

**Timing is not yet decided, and nothing before the cut depends on it.** November or early January, the work between now and then is identical: keep training, keep eating at maintenance-to-slight-surplus, pin the real TDEE. Decide in December. Starting after the holidays is the easier option and costs very little — you keep building through December instead.

### Training changes

Two governing rules:

1. **Cut volume, hold intensity.** Maintenance volume is a fraction of growth volume as long as loads stay heavy. Dropping to lighter weights and higher reps to "lean out" is the classic error and is how people lose muscle on a cut. Rep ranges stay exactly as written.
2. **Cut unevenly.** Trim where you are already well served, protect the priorities. Quads get 12 sets and are not the aesthetic bottleneck; delts and triceps are. So legs and chest absorb most of the reduction.

| Session | Change |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | 4-day only, no Upper C-5. Deload every 4–5 weeks instead of 6. **Training max held flat** for the entire cut — no increases. Drop the optional 5s PRO back-off set. |
| **Lower A** | Leg Press 3→2 · Standing Calf Raise 3→2 · Squat, RDL, Hip Thrust unchanged |
| **Upper A** | Weighted Pull-Up 4→3 · Seated DB Shoulder Press 3→2 · Cable Lateral Raise 4→3 · EZ Bar Curl 4→3 · **drop Incline Cable Fly** · Skullcrusher unchanged |
| **Lower B** | Seated Leg Curl 3→2 · Leg Extension 3→2 · Seated Calf Raise 3→2 · Pendulum/Hack Squat and SL-RDL unchanged |
| **Upper B** | Incline DB Press 4→3 · Incline Cable Fly 3→2 · Cable Face Pull 3→2 · Cable Lateral Raise 4→3 · Hammer Curl 3→2 · Pull-Up, Row, Overhead Extension unchanged |

Roughly a 20% volume reduction. Sessions land at 55–70 min.

- *Holding the TM flat is not optional — it is Wendler's own protocol for cutting. 5s PRO with no TM increases is already the correct 5/3/1 variant for a deficit, which is why the strength template does not need to change.*

### Nutrition during the cut

- **Deficit:** 15–20% below *measured* TDEE. Off a real number near 2,900 that is ~2,300–2,450 kcal, or about 1–1.25 lb/week. Do not go steeper — past ~1% bodyweight per week you start paying in lean mass.
- **Protein up to 190–215g/day.** Deficits raise protein requirements; the 170–190 range above was set for maintenance.
- **Fat floor ~60g/day.** Do not drop below this chasing calories.
- **Carbs take the remainder**, weighted toward pre- and post-lift meals. Those two meals are the ones to protect.

### Cardio during the cut

**Do not add any.** Cutting calories and adding cardio taxes recovery from both ends. If another lever is needed, raise the daily step target to 12–15k before adding a run.

### What to expect

A cut is a maintenance phase for muscle. Lifts stalling or drifting slightly down is normal and is not a reason to change anything. Red flags that mean back off the deficit, not the training:

- More than ~10% lost on bench or squat top sets
- Averaging more than 1.5 lb/week of bodyweight loss

Optional maintenance weeks at Thanksgiving and Christmas — take them for sanity and social reasons. Thanksgiving week is a convenient place to put a deload.

**Current phase (through Oct 2026): maintenance to slight surplus — slow lean gain.** This is what has actually been running for the past six months, and it is working. Keep it until the cut starts.

- Protein: 170–190g/day.
- Calories: at or slightly above maintenance. Do not cut yet.
- Pre-lift (60–90 min before): 30–50g carbs + 30g protein, low fat.
- Post-lift (within 90 min): 40–60g carbs + 40g protein.

**Pin your real TDEE before the cut.** The 1.65 multiplier above is an estimate, not a measurement. Change nothing about what you eat — just log weekly *average* bodyweight (not daily) for three weeks and read the trend:

| Weekly trend | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| +0.2 to +0.3 lb/wk | ~150–200 kcal above maintenance — true maintenance is current intake minus that |
| Flat | Current intake *is* maintenance |
| Losing | Already in a deficit — recalibrate before cutting further |

This takes three weeks, costs nothing, and means the cut is calibrated off a real number instead of a formula.

**Running and yoga**

- Running 1x/week baseline (Saturday, 6–8 miles). Daily step target: 10–12k.
- Lower B on Thursday gives legs one full rest day (Friday) before the Saturday long run.
- Yoga on Wednesday. Avoid intense hot yoga the night before Lower A or Lower B.
- On deload weeks, keep the Saturday run easy and short (30–40 min).

**Ceiling for added cardio and yoga**

- **Max 3 hard non-lifting sessions/week** (running + power/hot yoga combined). Restorative or yin yoga does not count toward this.
- **Running ≤2x/week and ≤15 miles/week.**
- **Stacking onto lifting days is correct.** Doubling up protects true rest days; adding separate days is what erodes recovery. Cardio goes *after* lifting, or separated by 6+ hours.
- **Never** hard running or hot yoga the day before Lower A or Lower B.
- Treat power/hot yoga as a training session, not recovery — it is bodyweight pressing volume plus heat stress.
- The count matters less than these signals. Back off if: squat or leg press loads stall 2+ weeks, legs feel heavy during warm-ups, resting HR drifts up, or sleep degrades.

**Core**

10 min after Lower A and Upper B. Two quality sessions beat four half-finished ones — skipping on a tired day is not a failure, it is why the schedule is two days.

- **Prioritize loaded flexion:** weighted cable crunch, weighted decline sit-up, ab wheel. This is what actually grows the rectus abdominis, and it progresses like any other lift.
- Planks, hollow holds and side planks are anti-extension endurance work. Useful, but they stop driving growth fairly quickly — do not let them be the whole session.
- Progress them: add load or reps, do not just add time.
- Visible abs are overwhelmingly a bodyfat question. Training makes them thicker; the cut makes them visible.

**Tracking**

- Weekly *average* bodyweight, not daily readings.
- Waist measured at the navel, same time of day.
- Photos monthly, same lighting and time of day.

At six months in, the mirror is unreliable and the scale alone is noise. These three together are not.

**Plateau management**

- For double-progression lifts: if a weight stalls for 2+ weeks through a deload, drop load 10% and rebuild.
- For 5s PRO lifts: if bench or squat top sets feel consistently above RPE 8 (< RIR 2) for 2+ weeks after a deload, reset training max by 10% and rebuild.
